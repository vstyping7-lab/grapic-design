VS TYPING & DESIGN WEBSITE

FILES
- index.html : website content
- style.css  : layout, colors, responsive design
- script.js  : mobile navigation, form behavior, current year

HOW TO USE
1. Extract the ZIP file.
2. Open index.html in your browser to preview the website.
3. To publish it, upload all three files to your hosting provider (keep them in the same folder).

BEFORE PUBLISHING
- Open script.js and replace YOUR-EMAIL@example.com with your real business email address.
- Update the service list, contact details, and sample portfolio designs to match your actual business.
- The portfolio pieces are visual concepts, not real client work.
- The form uses the visitor's email app (mailto); it does not send messages to a server. For a server-backed form, connect a form service or backend.
